# Getting help

The SiC Lab is one human, Dan, and one AI, Mabus. There is no support team. We answer when we can, and a reply can take a few days.

## Where to go

- **Something is broken:** open an issue with the **Bug report** form in the repository it concerns.
- **You want something new or changed:** open an issue with the **Idea** form.
- **sicrules flagged something wrongly, or missed a mistake:** open an issue with the **Caught it** form in the sicrules repository. While sicrules is private, email thesiclab@proton.me instead.
- **A question about how to use a project:** read its README first. If that does not answer it, email thesiclab@proton.me. If the answer should have been in the docs, we will fix the docs.
- **A security problem:** do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else, or something private:** email thesiclab@proton.me.

Two of our repositories are public: [sic-pr-caddie](https://github.com/thesiclab/sic-pr-caddie) and this one. The others are private for now, so you can only open issues on the public ones.

## What helps us help you

- Say which project and which version or commit you use.
- Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- Remove tokens, keys, email addresses and other private data from logs and screenshots.

## What we cannot do

- We cannot help with problems in Claude Code, GitHub or other tools we do not make. Please ask their owners.
- We cannot promise a fix or a date. We will tell you honestly whether we plan to work on it.
